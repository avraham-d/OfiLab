THIRD-PARTY NOTICES — ofiLab
============================

1. Questionnaire items (IPIP)
   International Personality Item Pool (IPIP), Lewis R. Goldberg / Oregon Research Institute.
   The original English items and scale keys are in the public domain:
   https://ipip.ori.org/newPermission.htm

   - IPIP-NEO-120: Johnson, J. A. (2014). Measuring thirty facets of the Five Factor Model
     with a 120-item public domain inventory: Development of the IPIP-NEO-120.
     Journal of Research in Personality, 51, 78–89. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jrp.2014.05.003
   - IPIP-NEO-300: https://ipip.ori.org/newNEOKey.htm (30 facets, 10 items each).
   This is the public-domain IPIP representation, not the proprietary NEO PI-R instrument.

   The English wording and the facet/key position of every item are kept beside the Hebrew
   text in data/ipip-neo-120-he.json and data/ipip-neo-300-he.json (source_en, source_id).
   The Hebrew translations are independent and experimental; they have not been
   psychometrically validated in Hebrew.

2. Norm tables (aggregate counts only, no respondent records)
   - IPIP-NEO-120: J. A. Johnson's original archive, https://osf.io/tbmh5/
     (N = 410,376 complete respondents) → data/empirical-norms.json, assets/empirical-data.js
   - IPIP-NEO-300: Johnson IPIP-NEO-300 data as published at
     https://huggingface.co/datasets/ecorbari/IPIP300-SCORES
     (N = 145,388 complete respondents) → data/empirical-norms-300.json, assets/empirical-data-300.js
   Derived parameters (assets/empirical-deep-models.js, assets/irt-facet-score-ci.js,
   assets/profile-links-data.js) are aggregate statistics computed from the same sources.

3. Supplementary questionnaires
   - HEXACO-H (16 items): items written for this site after the Honesty-Humility facets of the
     HEXACO model (Lee & Ashton, 2004). Not the official HEXACO-PI-R.
   - IPIP-VIA-R (60 items): after the public-domain IPIP-VIA scales (Bluemke, Partsch,
     Saucier & Lechner, 2021; McGrath, Hall-Simmonds & Goldberg, 2020).
   - ECR-RS (9 items): Fraley, Heffernan, Vicary & Brumbaugh (2011), Psychological Assessment.
