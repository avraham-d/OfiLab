THIRD-PARTY NOTICES — ofiLab
============================

1. Questionnaire items (IPIP)
   International Personality Item Pool (IPIP), Lewis R. Goldberg / Oregon Research Institute.
   The original English items and scale keys are in the public domain:
   https://ipip.ori.org/newPermission.htm

   Goldberg, L. R., Johnson, J. A., Eber, H. W., Hogan, R., Ashton, M. C., Cloninger,
   C. R., & Gough, H. G. (2006). The International Personality Item Pool and the future
   of public-domain personality measures. Journal of Research in Personality, 40, 84–96.

   - IPIP-NEO-120: Johnson, J. A. (2014). Measuring thirty facets of the Five Factor Model
     with a 120-item public domain inventory: Development of the IPIP-NEO-120.
     Journal of Research in Personality, 51, 78–89. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jrp.2014.05.003
   - IPIP-NEO-300: https://ipip.ori.org/newNEOKey.htm (30 facets, 10 items each).
   This is the public-domain IPIP representation, not the proprietary NEO PI-R instrument.

   The English wording and the facet/key position of every item are kept beside the Hebrew
   text in data/ipip-neo-120-he.json and data/ipip-neo-300-he.json (source_en, source_id).
   The Hebrew translations are independent and experimental; they have not been
   psychometrically validated in Hebrew.

2. Norm tables (aggregate counts only, no respondent records)
   - IPIP-NEO-120: J. A. Johnson's original archive, https://osf.io/tbmh5/
     (N = 410,376 complete respondents) → data/empirical-norms.json, assets/empirical-data.js
   - IPIP-NEO-300: Johnson IPIP-NEO-300 data as published at
     https://huggingface.co/datasets/ecorbari/IPIP300-SCORES
     (N = 145,388 complete respondents) → data/empirical-norms-300.json, assets/empirical-data-300.js
   Derived parameters (assets/empirical-deep-models.js, assets/irt-facet-score-ci.js,
   assets/profile-links-data.js) are aggregate statistics computed from the same sources.

3. Supplementary questionnaires
   - Honesty-Humility (16 items): items of the public-domain IPIP-HEXACO scales,
     https://ipip.ori.org/newHEXACO_PI_key.htm — Ashton, M. C., Lee, K., & Goldberg, L. R.
     (2007). The IPIP–HEXACO scales: An alternative, public-domain measure of the
     personality constructs in the HEXACO model. Personality and Individual Differences,
     42, 1515–1526. The HEXACO model: Lee, K., & Ashton, M. C. (2004). Multivariate
     Behavioral Research, 39, 329–358. Not the HEXACO-PI-R.
   - ECR-RS (9 items): not covered by the ofiLab license; it remains subject to its
     authors' terms. Hebrew translation of the items of Fraley, R. C., Heffernan, M. E.,
     Vicary, A. M., & Brumbaugh, C. C. (2011). The Experiences in Close Relationships —
     Relationship Structures questionnaire. Psychological Assessment, 23, 615–625.
   - Character strengths (60 items): written for this site, inspired by the public-domain
     IPIP-VIA-R scales (Bluemke, Partsch, Saucier & Lechner, 2021; McGrath,
     Hall-Simmonds & Goldberg, 2020). They are not the official IPIP-VIA-R items and are
     not validated. "VIA" is a trademark of the VIA Institute on Character; this site is
     not affiliated with it.

   Other models computed from the Big Five scores (no items reused):
   - Cybernetic Big Five Theory: DeYoung, C. G. (2015). Journal of Research in
     Personality, 56, 33–58.
   - UPPS impulsivity: Whiteside, S. P., & Lynam, D. R. (2001). Personality and
     Individual Differences, 30, 669–689.
   - Style graphs: after the style graphs of Costa & McCrae (1992).
   - Four-letter estimate: an approximation from Big Five facets, not the MBTI.

   Trademarks: MBTI and Myers-Briggs are trademarks of The Myers-Briggs Company; NEO PI-R
   is a trademark of PAR, Inc. They are used here only to describe what this site is not;
   ofiLab is not affiliated with these companies.
